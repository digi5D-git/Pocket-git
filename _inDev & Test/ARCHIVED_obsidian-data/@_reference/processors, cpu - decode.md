## AMD CPUs

The rules of processor naming between **Intel** & **AMD** are almost exactly the same, exception being AMD's *model names*.

**Example AMD Processor Name:** R7 7900X

> [!learn] AMD models
> - Ryzen 3 : Entry Level, suitable for everyday office work, watching videos, & light gaming
> - Ryzen 5 : Mainstream level, suitable for moderate gaming & general content creation
> - Ryzen 7 : Near Flagship level, suitable for most AAA title games & moderate content creation
> - Ryzen 9 : Flagship level, suitable for all AAA title games, heavy content creation, & streaming

> [!learn] AMD Processor Suffixes
> - **F** : Indicates processor does not have an integrated graphics core.
> - **X** : Represents high performance, higher clock speeds & improved performance
> - **X3D** : Same performance as the **X** series but adds AMD 3D V-Cache technology, offering higher performance in gaming compared to standard X series.
