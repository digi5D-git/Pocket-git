# Eisenhower Matrix

-- | Urgent | Not Urgent
---: | :---: | :---:
Important | Do | Schedule
Not Important | Delegate | Delete

## URGENT + IMPORTANT
*To do **immediately.***
- [ ] Task 1

## URGENT + NOT IMPORTANT
*Delegate the task or do it at the end of the **URGENT + IMPORTANT** tasks.*

### IMPORTANT + NOT URGENT
*Schedule when to do it. Then do it.*

### NOT URGENT + NOT IMPORTANT
*Do it later or delete it.*
