# Decimal Time

Scientists often record time as decimal. For example, decimal days divide the day into 10 equal 
parts, and decimal years divide the year into 10 equal parts. Decimals are easier to plot than both (a) minutes and seconds, which uses the sexagesimal numbering system, (b) hours, months and days, which has irregular month lengths. In astronomy, the so-called Julian day uses decimal days centered on Greenwich noon.

>[!hot] Seconds in a Decimal Minute
> Since there are 60 seconds in a minute, a tenth part represents ⁠60/10 = 6 seconds.
