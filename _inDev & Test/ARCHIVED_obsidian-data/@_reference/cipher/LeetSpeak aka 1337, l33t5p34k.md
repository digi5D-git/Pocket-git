# LeetSpeak Aka 1337, l33t5p34k

**Leet**, or **1337** is a form of coded writing with letters replaced by numbers or symbols graphically similar to common alphanumerica characters.

> [!info] Origin  
> Term *leet speak* comes from English *elite speak* & roughly translates to "language of the elite". As opposed to those who do not understand aka *neophytes*, or noobs

### Basics

**Leet Speak** language consists of replacing the common characters, letters A-Z & occasionally 0-9, by others with similar writing. In most basic versions, the *leet* replaces the letter **E** with the number **3**, the letter **O** with the number **0**, letters **I** & **L** with the number **1**, letter **S** with number **5**, & the letter **T** by the number **7**. Hence the word *LEET* equals *1337*.

--- 

In some advanced versions *leet* offers character combinations for a single letter.
